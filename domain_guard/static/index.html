THE BASICS

What does DomainGuard check?

DNS and email records online. Website connection and certificate checks are available in the local scanner.

01 / DNS

Where things go.

DNS is the address book for your domain. We look at where your website and email point, and the other records your providers publish.

Read the methodology ↗
02 / HTTPS

How visitors connect.

HTTPS encrypts the connection to your site. The local Python scanner inspects its certificate and visible browser security settings. These checks are not available in online DNS mode.

Read the methodology ↗
03 / EMAIL

Who can send as you.

Email authentication policies help receiving servers identify messages sent using your domain. We look at the records, not actual message delivery.

HELP & METHODOLOGY

How to read your report

How the checks work

The local Python scanner screens resolved addresses before connecting to public HTTPS endpoints. The hosted DNS mode queries Google Public DNS and does not contact the submitted website. Certificates are verified against the requested hostname. Redirects are not followed. A single response cannot establish the security of every page. Email records are observations, not full standards-compliance checks.

What if something is missing?

Not every domain needs every record or security header. A missing record may be intentional. Follow the recommendations that apply to your setup and ask your provider before changing DNS or security policies.

Is a completed report proof that my website is secure?

No. DomainGuard checks a limited set of publicly visible settings. It does not test your application code, passwords, databases or all possible attacks. For a full assessment, consult a qualified security professional.
