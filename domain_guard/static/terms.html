1. What DomainGuard does

DomainGuard provides limited observations of public domain configuration, including DNS, HTTPS, TLS, response headers and published email policies. The demonstration uses synthetic data. Production online checks inspect public DNS and published email records via a third-party DNS resolver. A disabled-by-default protected preview pilot may assess HTTPS, TLS and headers only for domainguard-check.vercel.app; general website checks require the explicitly enabled local software. The project does not provide a penetration test, certification, guarantee, legal advice or a substitute for professional security assessment.

3. Results and changes

A successful check does not prove a website is secure. Review recommendations against your own configuration and test changes with your provider before applying them.
