Privacy notice

Last updated: November 27, 2026

1. Scope and responsibility

DomainGuard is a personal software project developed by Tarun Puri in Alberta, Canada. This notice describes the static demonstration and the loopback-only version of the software. A publicly hosted live scanning service is not currently available. A separate notice and deployment review are needed before that changes.

2. Static demonstration

The application uses fixed example results. It does not submit entered domain names to a scan API, create user accounts, set application cookies or use analytics or advertising scripts. The font is served locally when installed. A hosting provider may independently process IP addresses, browser information, request timestamps and access logs to deliver and secure the site. Its own privacy terms govern that processing. Hosting arrangements and retention must be confirmed when the demonstration is deployed.

3. Local scanning

When explicitly enabled on your computer, the local server receives the submitted hostname. It temporarily holds scan results, random access credentials, scan times and rate-limit records in memory. The server does not intentionally log submitted domains, scan IDs or bearer credentials. It retains no permanent application scan history.

To perform checks, the local scanner sends DNS queries to its configured resolver and bounded HTTPS and TLS connections to the screened public destination. These services may observe the queried name, your network address, connection times and the scanner user agent. They may retain information under their own policies. DomainGuard does not control them.

4. Retention, access and deletion

Local jobs expire 15 minutes after submission and are removed by periodic cleanup. The in-memory rate-limit history is retained for its bounded rate window. Stopping the local server clears its application memory. Results displayed in the browser and files you copy, print or download remain under your control until you close or clear them. Do not share private results or credentials.

5. Safeguards and limits

Local scans use randomly generated access credentials, loopback restrictions, bounded requests and verified TLS for outbound HTTPS. These reduce risk but do not guarantee confidentiality or eliminate all vulnerabilities. The loopback development server must not be exposed through a public proxy or tunnel. Other processes on your device and your browser may have access to local information.

6. Questions and privacy rights

For a privacy question or a request about information the project operator controls, contact the maintainer through the GitHub profile. Do not post personal information or sensitive security details in public issues. A dedicated private privacy contact and any applicable provider disclosures must be established before a public live scanning service is launched. Applicable privacy rights are not waived by this notice.

This notice will be reviewed when the application's processing, hosting or retention changes. The date above identifies this version. See the Terms of use for permitted use and assessment limitations.
